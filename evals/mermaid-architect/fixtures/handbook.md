# Support Handbook

This handbook explains how the support team helps customers.

## Channels

Customers reach support by phone, email, or chat. Gold plan customers get the phone line. Basic plan customers use the email form.

## Escalation

An agent answers first. If the agent cannot solve the problem within one day, the agent hands it to a tier 2 specialist. A specialist who cannot solve it hands it to engineering.

### Tier 2

Tier 2 specialists own the case until engineering accepts it. They update the customer every day.

## Refunds

Agents may refund up to 50 dollars alone. Larger refunds need approval from a team lead. Every refund is logged in the billing system.
