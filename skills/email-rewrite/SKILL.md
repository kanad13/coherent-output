# Professional Email Rewrite

Follow this protocol to transform unstructured notes or rough drafts into clear, persuasive, and actionable emails.

---

## 1. Input Analysis & Clarification Gate

- **Inputs Required:** Source text/notes, recipient relationship, desired action, and known tone constraints.
- **Clarification Gate:** If an unknown fact materially alters the commitment, schedule, or core message, ask **exactly one focused question** and pause. In all other cases, proceed under stated reasonable assumptions.

---

## 2. Four-Step Drafting Protocol

### Step 1: Build the Communication Brief

Extract and inspect:

- **Sender Purpose:** Core objective of sending the message.
- **Recipient Context:** Reader's role, relationship, and decision authority.
- **Requested Action:** Concrete next step requested of the recipient.
- **Commitments & Dates:** Deadlines, deliverables, and dependencies.
- **Tone:** Professional, direct, sensitive, or assertive.

### Step 2: Formulate the Message Plan

Sequence the information in optimal reader-first order:

1. **Opening:** Core purpose or requested decision in the opening sentence.
2. **Context & Facts:** Background details, data, and constraints.
3. **Action & Next Steps:** Specific requests, owners, and timelines.
4. **Closing:** Polite, professional sign-off without unnecessary filler.

### Step 3: Draft the Email

- Write a specific, informative subject line indicating topic and action.
- Use natural, professional correspondence prose that is compliant with ASD-STE100 plain-language standards.
- Write in active voice with explicit actors and dates.

### Step 4: Audit & Deliver

- Verify that 100% of facts, names, commitments, and deadlines are preserved.
- Ensure all passive phrasing, apologetic hedges, and corporate clichés are eliminated.
- Deliver the final draft accompanied by a brief summary of tone choices.
