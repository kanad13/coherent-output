Ordinary FTS5 searches tokens. Its trigram tokenizer supports substrings, but `MATCH` returns no matches for queries shorter than three Unicode characters; short `LIKE`/`GLOB` searches need scan fallback. FTS5 therefore cannot replace scanning for your full requirement. [SQLite FTS5 documentation](https://www.sqlite.org/fts5.html#the_trigram_tokenizer)

Plain SQLite `LIKE` also folds case only for ASCII by default. It needs escaping to treat `%` and `_` literally. [SQLite expression documentation](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_expr.html#like)
